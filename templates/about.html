How CineMatch works for you

Four simple steps from "I don't know what to watch" to a movie night plan.

  1. 1

    Search for a movie you like

    Type a title, an actor or a director into the search bar at the top of any page. Suggestions appear as you type, so you can jump straight to the movie you mean.

    Go to the home page
  2. 2

    Browse and filter the catalog

    Not sure what you're in the mood for? Explore the full collection and narrow it down by genre, decade and minimum rating, then sort by what's popular or best rated.

    Open Explore
  3. 3

    See movies picked for you

    Open any movie to read its story, cast and rating. Right below it you'll find similar movies worth watching next. You can also choose a favorite on the Recommendations page and get a fresh list instantly.

    Open Recommendations
  4. 4

    Save it for later

    Tap the watchlist button on any movie to bookmark it. Your saved movies are waiting on the Watchlist page whenever you're ready, and you can remove them once you've watched them.

    Open Watchlist

Common questions

Quick answers before you start.

Is CineMatch free?

Yes. Searching, browsing, recommendations and your account are all free to use.

Do I need an account to get recommendations?

No. Pick any movie and you'll see similar suggestions straight away. An account is only needed to save a watchlist, rate movies and set favorite genres.

What kind of movies can I find?

There's a wide mix, from well-known classics to smaller films you may not have heard of.

Why can't I find a movie I'm looking for?

The catalog doesn't include every movie ever made. Try searching by the director or an actor's name, or check the spelling of the title.

How do I remove a movie from my watchlist?

Open your Watchlist page and use the remove button on the movie, or tap the watchlist button again on the movie's own page.
